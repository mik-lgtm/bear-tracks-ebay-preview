Available tread patterns

Select your tread pattern from the options on this listing. Availability can vary by brand.

C-Lug
A versatile all-purpose pattern with strong traction and a smooth ride on mixed terrain. A great default for general construction.
Block
Built for hard surfaces like asphalt, concrete, and compacted ground, with reduced vibration and long wear.
Staggered Block
Balances traction, ride comfort, and track life on hard ground and mixed jobsites.
Zig-Zag
Excellent for mud, snow, and wet clay. The angled lugs help shed debris and keep gripping.
Multi-Bar
Aggressive traction in loose soil, mud, and snow, while spreading the load to limit ground disturbance.
Straight Bar
Maximum pulling power in soft or muddy ground, for when traction matters more than ride comfort.
Turf
Low-profile tread that minimizes damage to lawns, sod, and finished surfaces. Ideal for landscaping.
